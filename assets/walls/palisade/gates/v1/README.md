# Closed palisade gates — stage 1 (south-east and south-west)

**Pending visual approval.** Only `gate_dir2.png` and `gate_dir3.png` are replaced in this stage. The reference artwork is the accepted `edge_dir2.webp` and `edge_dir3.webp` from PR #182, commit `c2831d0b4709fff10a6293bb7bbd20e110964f43`. The two candidates are independent AI-generated raster images, technically calibrated against those references by whole-image projective registration, alpha cleanup and modest palette adjustment. No wall pixels or different gate graphics are composited into them.

Both files are 423 × 444 RGBA PNGs, with the existing render anchors unchanged. Opaque interiors were verified, and the sprites were examined against light, dark and green backgrounds. Outer stake textures and individual silhouette steps are redrawn by the model, so these test candidates are not pixel-identical to the accepted walls. The end-stake foot positions are registered to within one raster pixel; visual continuity and any remaining differences require editor review.

Directions 0, 1, 4 and 5 retain their previous images pending stage 2 and are not approved; direction 5 intentionally does not yet reuse the new direction 2. Original palisades, masonry, stone gates, junctions, shadows and vegetation clearance logic are unchanged. Do not regenerate these two files with the historical v4 composition script or use those previous gate assets as new visual references.
