# Closed palisade gates — complete AI raster set, edge-on refinement v8

The active gate set uses AI raster artwork. Direction 5 reuses the exact direction 2 PNG bytes; directions 1 and 4 reuse the same edge-on v8 PNG, retaining their own existing anchors. The accepted original palisade edge WebPs are the reference artwork. No SVG, procedural gate drawing, or compositing of separate timber images is used in these candidates. Historical v4 composition scripts are not a source for the new set.

Original canvas dimensions and render anchors are retained: 420 × 444 px (0), 84 × 630 px (1, 4), 423 × 444 px (2, 3, 5). Technical preparation uses whole-image geometric calibration, alpha cleanup and modest palette matching. Directions 1 and 4 use one newly AI-edited edge-on sprite based on the uploaded original palisades, with all eight pointed tips and a flat foot retained, and only three subtle lateral wooden slat ends on each side. The sprite is reused without mirroring to retain consistent lighting; no front-facing gate panels, metal fittings or transverse bars are present. The original walls, stone assets, joints, shadows and vegetation clearance logic remain unchanged.

NE v7 replaces the earlier twelve-tip candidate with a newly generated ten-tip painting, following the original descending hex projection. Both outer foot points match the reference to within one raster pixel in Y; the two horizontal rails follow the descending edge and remain straight. All six candidate sprites have one connected alpha silhouette and no partially transparent interior pixels (two-pixel boundary excluded), and have been checked against light, dark and green backgrounds. Some texture, tip positions and internal baseline steps still differ from the original walls. Exact visual equivalence and complete browser/editor acceptance are not claimed.

Edge-on v8: 84 × 630 RGBA; occupied vertical span y=76–559 matches the original. The lateral detail widens the silhouette by roughly 3–4 raster pixels. The generated gate was cropped from a comparison image and calibrated as one complete raster, without compositing timber parts. Texture is AI-redrawn, not pixel-identical to the original. Actual editor visual approval remains pending.
