# Closed palisade gates — stage 2 (all directions)

All six directions now use the new AI-generated raster candidate set. Directions 0, 1 and 4 were independently generated using the accepted original palisade references; directions 2 and 3 retain the previously integrated stage 1 PNGs. Direction 5 is a byte-identical reuse of direction 2 with the existing direction 5 render anchor. No SVG, drawing of gate components, or compositing of different timber images is used in the new assets.

The original reference canvas sizes and render anchors are retained: 420 × 444 px (0), 84 × 630 px (1, 4), 423 × 444 px (2, 3, 5). Technical preparation comprises whole-image alignment, alpha cleanup, modest palette correction, and a small central width calibration for the two edge-on sprites. The existing normal palisades, stone artwork, joins, shadows and vegetation clearance are not modified.

Stage 2 QA: the new independent sprites have opaque interiors and transparent exteriors. They were compared with their original references against light, dark and green backgrounds. Direction 0 has 12 rather than 10 visible stake tips, and the wood texture and internal stake positions are not pixel-identical to the originals. Directions 1 and 4 are separately painted, not flipped, with a narrow reinforced gate assembly. These are review candidates, not a claim of exact visual equivalence or final editor approval. Historical v4 composition outputs and scripts are not a source for this set. Do not regenerate this set using those scripts.
